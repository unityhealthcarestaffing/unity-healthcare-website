Our standards

Our commitment to safe, high-quality care

Safeguarding and compliance sit at the heart of everything we do. We work in partnership with care homes, supported living services, hospitals and community providers to supply reliable staff who meet UK expectations and deliver care with dignity, respect and competence.

Robust recruitment & vetting

All workers must complete our compliance checks before any shifts are offered.

  • Enhanced DBS (role-dependent)
  • Right to work verification
  • Identity verification
  • Employment history review
  • Professional references
  • Qualifications / experience checks

Mandatory training

We support role-appropriate training and encourage ongoing development.

  • Safeguarding awareness
  • Infection prevention & control
  • Health & safety
  • Manual handling
  • Equality & diversity
  • Mental Capacity Act awareness

Quality assurance

We maintain high standards through monitoring, feedback and swift action where needed.

  • Up-to-date compliance records
  • Shift performance monitoring
  • Client feedback & follow-up
  • Incident and concern handling
  • Support for staff and clients

Professional conduct

We set clear expectations for punctuality, communication, confidentiality and respectful care.

  • Dignity and respect always
  • Follow care plans & site policies
  • Clear communication and handover
  • Accurate documentation where required
  • Escalate concerns promptly
Safeguarding & reporting: Any safeguarding concern is treated seriously. If there is an immediate risk of harm, contact emergency services. For safeguarding concerns related to our staff or services, contact our team as soon as possible via info@unityhealthcarestaffing.co.uk or call +44 7542 123 984. Where appropriate, we may share relevant information with client organisations, safeguarding teams, regulators or authorities to protect individuals and meet legal obligations.

Data protection (UK GDPR)

We take confidentiality seriously and process personal data in line with UK GDPR requirements.

  • Secure systems and access control
  • Need-to-know information sharing
  • Audit-friendly record keeping
  • Responsible incident response

Supporting our clients

We work collaboratively with providers to match staff skills to service needs.

  • Clear requirements and expectations
  • Role and setting matching
  • Fast response where possible
  • Digital booking and records
Compliance queries: If you need compliance documentation, onboarding support, or have questions about our standards, contact us at info@unityhealthcarestaffing.co.uk or call +44 7542 123 984.

This page provides an overview of our approach and does not replace contractual terms or legal advice. Specific requirements may vary by role and client site.
